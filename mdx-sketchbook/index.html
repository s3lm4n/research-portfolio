PLATE I · A STUDY IN SPECIALISED HARDWARE · 2026

MDX

Molecular Dynamics Accelerator

A study of specialised hardware for the short-range nonbonded force: the pairwise calculation that a molecular simulation repeats at every timestep.

One nonbonded pair. MDX studies hardware for computing this force, pair after pair.
Selman Ali DokumacıResearcher
Newton’s third law: one evaluation, two atoms
PLATE II · THE LOOP

Every pair, every timestep.

Find neighbour pairs inside the cutoff.
Evaluate the Lennard-Jones force for each pair.
Add each pair’s force to both atoms.
Integrate positions, advance one timestep, repeat.
Bonded terms, long-range electrostatics, constraints and integration stay in host software.
PLATE III · WHY THE PAIR LOOP

The same arithmetic, again, every timestep.

MDX starts from a premise: in many classical timesteps, short-range nonbonded pair forces are a large share of the work, and the pair loop is regular and numerically intensive.

PREMISE · NOT AN MDX MEASUREMENT
NOT RUN ON MDX HARDWARE
10-bit IDs name 1,024 atoms · the 8B simulation
19-bit IDs: 524,288 · the nominal simulations
10 million atoms need ≥ 24 bits · not implemented
